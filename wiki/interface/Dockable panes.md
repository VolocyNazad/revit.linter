> Language: **English** · [[Закрепляемые панели|Русский]]

In Revit, **"Dockable panes"** are interface elements that can be freely moved, docked to the edges of the main program window, or floated (including on additional monitors). This allows you to flexibly customize your workspace to your preferences.
