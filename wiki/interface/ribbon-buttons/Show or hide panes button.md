# Show or hide panes button

The **Show/Hide** button opens or hides every Revit Linter dockable pane at once: diagnostic results, the fix list, the diagnostics list and the ignore list. When all of them are shown, the same button hides them. The practical-tour pane is shown and hidden by the tour itself.

The button sits on the **Diagnostics** tab.

Hover over the button to see what it does, and press **F1** while the tooltip is shown to open [[Dockable panes|Dockable panes]].
