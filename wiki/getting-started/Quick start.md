> Language: **English** · [[Быстрый старт|Русский]]

1. [[Installation|Install Revit Linter]] and restart Revit.
2. Open a project or family.
3. On the **Volocy** ribbon tab, open the [[Diagnostics pane|Diagnostics]] and [[Diagnostic reports pane|Diagnostic results]] panels.
4. In **Diagnostics**, enable the checks you need and adjust their severity when required.
5. Select the active document in **Diagnostic results**.
6. Press **Run**. Use the filters to narrow the results and click an element id to find it in Revit.

Revit normally docks the panels at the bottom of the window. You can move, resize, or float them like other [[Dockable panes|dockable panes]].
