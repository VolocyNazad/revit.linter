New built-in family checks: `SHRD0046` reports a saved family with 6 or more types beyond the default type, with or without a type catalog, and cites the Autodesk advice to use a catalog for such families; `SHRD0047` warns when the family keeps types that its type catalog does not list; `SHRD0048` reports a family that stores its own types inside the family and in a type catalog at the same time, counting only the types beyond the one Revit cannot delete. The type that carries the family name, or has a blank name, stands for the family itself and is never counted or reported. The catalog is the `.txt` file next to the `.rfa`, so an unsaved family and a family edited from a project have nothing to check.
